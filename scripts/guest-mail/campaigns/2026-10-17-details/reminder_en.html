Getting there
Staying on the mainland? Head to the Vinpearl terminal, take the cable car or ferry across (about 10 minutes), then the free Marriott shuttle to the resort. Allow 30 to 40 minutes door to door, and aim to arrive by 3:30 pm so you can settle in before the 4:00 pm ceremony.
